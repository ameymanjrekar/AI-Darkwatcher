How Are We Fixing These AI Problems?

Emerging safety solutions designed to stop bad AI actions

Active Defenses
🛡️ Digital Human Fingerprints (C2PA) STOPS DEEPFAKES
  • The Solution: Real cameras and mics now sign recordings with an unbreakable digital stamp.
  • How it Helps: Phones and apps will flag any video without this stamp as synthetic or fake.
🔒 Virtual Jails for Autonomous AI Bots (MicroVMs) STOPS ROGUE AGENTS
  • The Solution: AI agents are locked in temporary virtual rooms that self-destruct after every task.
  • How it Helps: Even if a bot goes rogue, it cannot touch real files, passwords, or the internet.
👮 Dual-Guard Security Checkpoints STOPS HACKING
  • The Solution: A second, independent "police AI" inspects every question and answer before execution.
  • How it Helps: Catches sneaky hidden hack prompts before the main AI ever sees them.
